## Methodological Update & Pipeline Evolution Summary

### Context and Rationale for dev2 Pipeline Re-Benchmarking

In our initial draft (developed on branch `main`), student dropout prediction was framed as a 'first-year early warning system' that incorporated cumulative Grade Point Average (`ira`). However, following the reviewer's incisive comments regarding temporal granularity, feature measurement windows, and potential target leakage (specifically Comments #10, #20, #28, and #63), we conducted a rigorous data provenance and timing audit on the institutional database (`dataset_evasao_MDE.xlsx`).

This audit revealed a critical structural reality: **`ira` (cumulative GPA), `reprovacoes` (course failures), and `percentual_frequencia` (attendance percentage) in the administrative records are cumulative summary variables computed across students' entire multi-year tenures up to their terminal outcome.** Treating cumulative GPA as a 'first-year' predictor introduced severe retrospective target leakage, artificially inflating recall (84.8%) and ROC-AUC (0.951).

### The Architectural Remediation (`dev2` Pipeline)

Rather than attempting to defend an ethically and methodologically flawed construct, we executed a complete architectural overhaul on branch `dev2`:
1. **Strict Elimination of Target Leakage:** Cumulative GPA (`ira`) was **completely excluded** from the feature set. Predictors are strictly restricted to verifiable baseline administrative and academic friction features prior to terminal departure.
2. **Leak-Free Cross-Validation Pipeline:** We instituted a 70/30 stratified train/test split ($N_{train} = 10,558$, $N_{test} = 4,526$; seed 42). To guarantee zero data leakage, all imputation, winsorization, one-hot encoding, feature selection, and resampling were encapsulated strictly inside 10-fold cross-validation training folds using `imblearn.pipeline.Pipeline`, preserving natural 22.4% dropout prevalence in validation folds.
3. **Locked dev2 Champion Model:** Under this leak-free protocol, the Random Forest champion achieves honest generalization: **Recall: 73.18%** (95% CI: [70.48%, 76.00%]), **Precision: 75.64%** (95% CI: [72.83%, 78.19%]), **Accuracy: 88.71%**, **ROC-AUC: 0.8968** (95% CI: [0.8836, 0.9098]), and **Brier Score: 0.1080** (vs. 0.1738 baseline) on $N=4,526$ ($TP=742, FP=239, TN=3,273, FN=272$).
4. **Recalculated Explainability & Fairness:** TreeSHAP attributions now prove that academic friction dominates: course failures (32.37%), attendance (19.26%), and cohort year (10.78%) drive 62.41% of total attribution. Algorithmic fairness audits across 5 axes at $\tau=0.50$ evaluate Disparate Impact under the Four-Fifths screening heuristic (0.80 <= DI <= 1.25) and apply Holm-Bonferroni correction to Equal Opportunity Difference (EOD) $p$-values. Pre-processing Reweighing was shown to drop recall by 12.82 pp ($73.18\% \rightarrow 60.36\%$), justifying the need for institutional human oversight.
5. **Impact on Review Suggestions:** Consequently, review comments that specifically analyzed old GPA SHAP percentages (~54%), old 84.8% recall, old 3,017-student numbers, or old SVM 91.4% recall are **superseded by the dev2 pipeline overhaul**. For full transparency, each point is itemized in the table below, with complete corrections for applicable points and explicit justifications for superseded points.
